# RCE Compile

RCE Compile is a research project that asks whether one Law of Compilation
holds for every compiler: a statement of what a correct, well-ranked
compiler must satisfy, against which gcc, rustc, Go, GHC, the tensor
compilers (compilers of array programs such as neural-network models) and
the rest can all be placed. A *compiler* here is any program
that turns a program text into another program text, down to machine code
or a lower representation.

## Status

Research, draft. The charter and the hypotheses are written and registered;
one experiment, E1 (determinism of lowering), has a harness and committed
results; E2 to E4 are protocols that have not been run. No law is claimed.

## Relation to RCE

RCE (the Reality Code Engine) is the language this repository defines.
RCE Compile is independent of it: it does not use RCE's checker, its Form
or its specification, and nothing in `compile/` builds or calls them. RCE's
own emitters are one subject among the many compilers the project can
measure, with no special place. The two share the repository and its
working rules, and the principle of deterministic work behind a gate.

## Principles

1. Deterministic first: at least 98% of the work done by deterministic
   means and at most 2% by stochastic ones, everything gated. The split is
   hypothesis H6, to be measured, not a claim.
2. Every rule is logical, from hindsight (what existing compilers show),
   physics and information theory.

[CHARTER.md](CHARTER.md) gives both in full, with the standard they lead to.
