# Code of conduct

This project is a place to learn, teach and build together. Everyone who
takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project
space, is expected to follow this code.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. People arrive with different backgrounds and
  different amounts of experience.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- Criticise ideas and code, never people.
- Give credit, and say where an idea or a piece of text came from.
- Accept a "no" to a change gracefully, and give reasons for your own.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual language or attention.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after you have been asked to stop.

## Scope

This code applies in every project space, and when someone represents the
project in public.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, write to **contact@openie.dev**. Reports
are read by the maintainers and kept confidential. You will get an answer,
and nobody will be penalised for a report made in good faith.

## What maintainers may do

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other
contributions that break this code, and may warn, suspend or ban a
participant, temporarily or permanently. They explain their decision to the
people involved.

## Attribution
